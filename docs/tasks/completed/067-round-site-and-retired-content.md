# 067 — Round site and retired content

## Objective / concept
- Playtest iteration on world/site, tool, controls and art chapters: a round meadow excavation, plain non-grass permanent walking ground and the existing stores. Remove the valley scenery and rejected custom assets/tools. Keep the current rock/mineral gameplay and custom-versus-pack soil comparison.
- Move direct sunlight overhead for midday, preserving excavation daylight attenuation underground.

## Live implementation
- MainGame owns a square voxel allocation and saved density/identities; the surrounding valley is generated by ReservoirEnvironmentSetup. Its scenery can be removed independently.
- SurfaceGrassRenderer checks nine support points per canopy, allowing small openings between them. Its change-driven batches already cover the canopy bounds.
- The experimental tool spans player/input/HUD/admin, shaped voxel cuts and a legacy v6 save slot. StarterFindSetup now imports active rocks/minerals; the empty starter source only holds compatibility aliases.

## Architecture / exact changes
- Add repeatable RoundSiteSetup: remove generated Environment and rectangular surface rims; generate a permanent annular gravel slab around a circular opening. Preserve the existing subsurface allocation and bedrock for lateral excavation beneath the permanent apron. Stations and return/recharge anchors remain on solid ground. The fixed apron stays above the shallowest saved find envelopes; bedrock walls meet its underside.
- SiteLayout owns the opening/apron dimensions. A serialized optional grass surface radius clips MainGame vegetation to the opening; small fixtures remain rectangular.
- Check every surface grid column intersecting a clump's conservative canopy plus wind margin. Reject the entire clump whenever any part overlaps an opening; recalculate on excavation and restore, with no saved vegetation state.
- Keep approved BK sky and wind; configure near-vertical sun. Delete custom grass/cloud/sun/tool-rig/earthshaper sources and their imported outputs and setup code. Remove generated project valley assets, preserving all vendor paths and metadata.
- Remove experiment enum, shaped cuts, rig renderer, admin controls, input action and HUD. Keep ordinary scoop behavior. Use current-only v7 checkpoints without experiment or fractional-money fields; reject older versions. Delete the old format readers and depth migration, retaining atomic current-format recovery.
- Rename the shared discovery importer and move the merged catalog with its GUID. Delete legacy-aliases.json, alias resolution and obsolete starter sources/import branch. Only current rocks/minerals remain.
- Update scene and regression checks to the new ownership; retire checks solely for removed features. Update current concept/baseline/architecture references.

## Edge cases
- Never access or rewrite user saves or the recovery scene. Tests use disposable state; visual verification uses additive MainGame with persistence disabled.
- Keep device preferences independent of world format. Current-format snapshots round-trip density, find identities and progression; older formats and mismatched layouts are rejected. The obsolete input preference field is ignored.
- Vegetation must clear at a patch border, at the round rim, for a small cut under a wide leaf, after restore and after deeper repeated strokes; intact distant plants remain.
- Permanent apron colliders must block digging from above without capping the circular opening. Mesh render/collision ownership must match.
