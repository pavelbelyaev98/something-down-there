# Architecture

- `unity/Assets/Runtime/Player` — input, movement, camera, battery, preferences, player state, wallet/trade/rescue, save control and session-only developer controls.
- `unity/Assets/Runtime/Interaction` — targeting, inventory identities, discovery placement, reveal/collection, find physics and crane salvage.
- `unity/Assets/Runtime/Terrain` — finite signed density and immutable material fields, smooth surface-net meshes, adaptive dig adapter, boundaries and derived surface grass/daylight.
- `unity/Assets/Runtime/UI` — HUD and menu behavior (UI Toolkit under `UI/Toolkit`).
- `unity/Assets/Runtime/Persistence` — versioned whole-world snapshots, atomic disk storage, recovery and autosave.
- `unity/Assets/Runtime/Validation` — disposable validation adapters, not production mechanics.
- `unity/Assets/Scenes`, `unity/Assets/Editor`, `unity/Assets/Tests` — scenes, editor tooling and repository-owned checks. `unity/Packages/manifest.json` is the dependency source of truth.
- `unity/Assets/Plugins/Sirenix` — approved Odin Inspector/Validator in Editor Only mode; `Assets/Editor/Validation/MainGameValidation.asset` owns game-content scan scope. Odin's build automation runs that profile before builds; game persistence remains independent.

- `MainGame.unity` owns one player/menu root and world camera. `MainGameRoot` owns excavation, the permanent rim/boundaries, the generated lakebed environment and station anchors. `ToolRigPresenter` (under the player camera, wired by `ToolRigSetup`) only presents the tool: it reads the effective level, motion and last dig material from `FpsPlayer` and never affects digging; level-driven shovel/drill motions share the existing player/terrain/grid pipeline.
- `DesktopInstance` reserves one Windows player before scene load; duplicate launches focus the existing window and exit. `WorldSaveStore` separately locks the profile. Saves live in `Application.persistentDataPath/Save` (Editor uses `EditorSave`); only the current save format is supported, with atomic replacement and a retained previous checkpoint. No previous-format readers or content aliases remain.
- `FpsPlayer` owns `CameraPreferences`, `InputPreferences` and `GamePreferences` (separate `Preferences/camera-v1.ini`, `input-v1.ini`, `game-v1.json`; `EditorPreferences` in the Editor). `GamePreferences` is the sole runtime display and graphics owner, applied through `UnityGameSettingsPlatform` onto a runtime URP clone, the view camera and `QualitySettings`; `DesktopWindow` supplies native defaults. `GraphicsQuality` defines presets; `FpsPlayer` owns the one-off `GraphicsAutoTuner`. `GraphicsQualitySetup` (editor) keeps renderer 1 a feature-free copy of renderer 0.
- `FpsHud` owns the single UI Toolkit document with `GameHudView`/`GameMenuView`; one retained EventSystem routes menu input; no Canvas HUD.
- `TerrainVolume`/`ExcavationGrid` own removed volume and synchronously rebuild matching render/collision meshes. `TerrainMaterialSnapshot` owns immutable material IDs on that same lattice (saved bytes: append new families, never renumber; `EquipmentProgression.HardnessOrder` defines hardness, not enum order); `TerrainGround` generates them from the seed (backfill pits with their stashes, geodes and caverns, which placement can query through `GroundLayout`), limited to the features `SiteLayout.Ground` admits to the site (other grids stay soil); a seeded grid carves the pockets of air its stash chests stand in (`TerrainGround.Stashes`, the pocket measured by `ChestSetup` and copied onto `TerrainVolume` by the discovery sync) and its geodes' hollows into untouched density and `TerrainVolume.SeededAir` builds their chunks; `GridSnapshot` shares them with the save worker. Adaptive brushes apply `EquipmentProgression.MaterialResponse` per sample; `FpsPlayer` applies contact cadence/fuel and `TerrainVolume.ToolCut` publishes committed feedback. Geometric rope/blast removal (`TerrainVolume.ClearLoadSweep`) stays independent of tool resistance. `TerrainVolume` owns reusable Burst meshing/exposure workspaces. `TerrainChunkMesh` rounds each hole's mouth (mesh and collider only, `ShapeMouths`) and derives one weight stream `(free, free, geode shell, 1 − backfill)` in UV2 from the material halo (later grounds fill the free slots), with soil as the remainder, so meshes without the stream read as soil; its density cache also tracks immutable material identity. `SiteLayout` owns the grid size, plot outline, find footprint and permanent bank; `ExcavationGrid` clamps tool cuts to the bank. `ExcavationDaylight` and `SunPresentationSetup` derive presentation without excavation authority and save no lighting state.
- `DensitySnapshot`/`PagedDensity` own density pages: a page holding one value throughout is a shared read-only array (flagged), copied before its first write, written to saves as a marker. `CavernScenery` (on the terrain) owns the crystal cavern's areas (`CavernFormation` scenery, `CavernCrystal` dig targets, the `CavernShatter` particles), lights and bloom, rebuilt from the ground layout, `CavernDressing` (built by `CavernSetup`, props copied by `BuriedPropsSetup`) and which pieces `DiscoveryField` still holds sealed; it saves nothing. `DiscoveryField` owns the sealed pieces (`FindState.Sealed`) and releases them.
- Approved vendor content stays under `Assets/BK` and `Assets/TowerCrane` (and the prop packs `Assets/NOT_Lonely`, `Assets/JustPlay`, `Assets/_Television_set`, `Assets/REAL_DEDICATED` and Crystal Caverns' crystals, whose URP copies `BuriedPropsSetup` writes to `Assets/Content/BuriedProps`); game material and texture overrides live under `Assets/Content/Nature` and `Assets/Content/Lakebed`. `NatureEnvironmentSetup` adds BK's environment manager for wind globals and the URP depth/opaque textures the pack shaders need; project systems retain lighting ownership.
- MainGame owns excavation and stations. `LakebedSiteSetup` regenerates `MainGameRoot/Environment`, the rim collar, the camp placement and the bedrock (`MainGameSceneBuilder.PlaceBedrock`) from the unmodified Highlands demo into `Assets/Content/Lakebed`; its partials own the lakebed dressing (`.Lakebed`), dig plot cap, band and clay-loam topsoil (`.DigGround`), the dig boundary (`.Boundary`), water shader and materials (`.Presentation`) and detail, caster and visibility settings (`.Performance`). `SunPresentationSetup` owns sky, sun, grading, shadows and reflections. `GroundTextureSetup` configures the dig ground materials that `GroundTriplanar` blends from mesh material weights. `WindowsBuild` always builds MainGame.
- `DiscoveryCatalog` and the `DiscoveryContentSetup` importers (`PhotoRockSetup`, `MineralSetup`, `RetroComputerSetup`, `PropFindSetup`, `ChestSetup`) keep item identities separate from prefab art; finds made from bought prop prefabs bake through `PropBake`. They generate each find mesh's Mesh LOD levels after its exposure samples (`GenerateDetailLevels`). `DiscoveryField` reserves authored unique envelopes before seeded commons and owns the stash chests (`BuriedChest`: its own pose, fall, opened lid and break-in, saved as `ChestSnapshot`s; the break-in's clods and dust reuse the crane's ground-break effects through `SalvageCrane.EmitGroundBreak`); what a chest holds is ordinary population seated in it (`DiscoveryCatalog.ChestHeap`), and so are a geode's crystals (`SeatGeodes`, entries flagged `Geode`). `DiscoveryField` also owns whether each geode is open, derived from the grid on every load and set by the first cut whose way reaches a hollow. `BuriedFind` owns one `FindState` record through world, extraction, storage and display; bag collection is common-only, and a hand-picked find is taken only through its `TryInteract`. It also owns its transform's `hasChanged` flag, which gates reuse of its checkpoint record (`CaptureCheckpoint`, shared with the save worker and never edited); nothing else may reset it. `Capture()` returns an editable copy.
- `FpsPlayer` owns `FindDetector`; `DiscoveryField.PopulationRevision` invalidates its authored-source cache. `DetectorTargeting` selects by aim alignment within range and returns three signal levels with small angular/range hysteresis, tuned in `EquipmentProgression`. `FindDetector` excludes any exposed or previously observed find using existing `BuriedFind` state. `GameHudView` drives the separate three-bar panel directly; detector state is derived and unsaved.
- `HoldInteraction` owns armed hold-Interact targeting in `FpsPlayer` for every `IHoldTarget` (a unique's crane mark, a chest's lock). `SalvageCrane` owns the single recovery job (mark, route planning, the reusable `RecoveryMarkView` glyph and lifting eye, delivery, save capture/restore); `ExtractionRoutePlanner` searches immutable density incrementally; `CraneRopeView` owns the crane's cable, drawn as the pack's two falls (hexagonal strands rebuilt each frame around the rope's centre line), and the smart rope's world collision queries (straight from the trolley's sheaves to the hook block while hanging; during a recovery simulating the rope below the straight shaft and drawing it straight up the shaft above, carrying the released hook at its end, kept square to the trolley's sheaves, and seating it in the lifting eye); `RopeDynamics` integrates bounded rope particles, distance constraints, momentum-preserving payout/retraction and contact response. `SalvageCrane.Physics` retains the load spring and reel-speed/tension burst, then hangs the load from the hook's seat by a jointed link until the set-down; `SalvageCrane.Contacts` gates local breaks through `TerrainVolume`/`ExcavationGrid.RemoveBoxSweep`; `SalvageCrane.Feedback` throws pooled debris after committed breaks (clods landed by marching the ground's density, crumbs, dust, a delayed trickle; `SoilBreak.shader` lights them like the excavation), and the lingering shaft dust, switched by `FpsPlayer`'s session admin flag; `GroundLab.Crane` lays out the development Ground Lab's crane scenes (carved as `ExcavationGrid.LabCarve`, computer and rock poses that `DiscoveryField.UseGroundLab` spawns; `WorldSaveController` opens, restarts and leaves the lab); `SalvageCrane.Operator` works the crane's levers (over the hole mouth, lift, carry, set-down). `TowerCraneRig` owns the purchased pack's rig: the pack's control model (relative torques/forces and the hoist joint's linear limit, per physics step) behind four levers, an anti-sway assist, releasing the hook to the rope / placing it / seating it back on the hoist, where the cable's two falls leave the sheaves and enter the block, and the hook's seat, geometry queries and `CranePose` apply/capture. `FindPhysics` yields body control, forwards recovery contacts and lets the player through commons only; `LoadRide` (in `FpsPlayer`'s move) carries a player standing on a moving unique and pushes one it moves into. The checkpoint (`ExtractionSnapshot`) retains the accepted route, load pose/velocities, guide progress and the crane's pose; rope oscillation is transient.
- `SalvageCraneSetup` builds `Content/Salvage/SalvageCrane.prefab`, a variant of the vendor `TC_old_Base_small` (URP material copies in `Content/Salvage/Crane`, vendor `Controller_TC`/`Hook` scripts removed, hook colliders off, slewing colliders on Ignore Raycast, a copy of the jib mesh without the hoist cable), measures the cable's two falls (ends, spacing, strand radius, material and texture strip) and the hook's seat, and places it, its rope view (`RopeSettings.asset`, the jib's own material), the lifting eye model and the set-down spots in MainGame; `SalvageCraneValidator` checks the wiring before builds. Vendor files under `Assets/TowerCrane` stay untouched ([card](../art/tower-crane/README.md)).
- `WorksiteTools` owns placed lamps, the placement solver and previews, the nearest-lamps light budget and projected `WorldMark` stencils, and armed C4 charges (`PlacedCharge`), their preview and detonation (`WorksiteTools.Charges`, blasting through `TerrainVolume.Blast`); `FpsPlayer.LampKit` owns how many lamps the player owns, and `StationTrade.LampOffer` sells one more; `FpsPlayer.Charges` (`ChargeKit`) owns the C4 track and the charges owned, sold by `StationTrade.ChargeOffer`. `ChargeSetup` builds the charge prefab and preview material. `WorkLamp` owns support release, physical motion, its light fade and where it shines from; terrain edit bounds trigger support checks. `WorksiteSnapshot` persists equipment with the hole. `WorksiteToolsSetup` wires MainGame and original `art/work-lamps` content; `GamePreferences` preserves lamp occlusion independently of optional sun shadows.
- `ComputerStation` switches the shared terminal from selling to upgrades; `StationTrade` binds trades to inventory/wallet/battery revisions; `EquipmentProgression` owns the shared level ceiling, tool profiles, shovel/drill milestone, capacity increments and price ladder. `ShovelState` owns purchased tool progress; save restore derives its motion from that level.

Update this file only when folder ownership, major system boundaries or scene ownership changes.
