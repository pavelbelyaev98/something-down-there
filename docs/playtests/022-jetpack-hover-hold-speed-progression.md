# Playtest 022 — Jetpack Speed Progression

**Build & start:** current build, New Game. Developer admin → Add $500 helps buy Jetpack levels quickly.

## Try
- Fly home from depth on the starter jetpack, then buy Jetpack levels at the computer and fly again.
- Fly up a narrow shaft and land at the rim. Flight is Space only: hold to climb, let go to fall.

## Good feels like
- Stable and easy at every level; each purchase climbs clearly faster and drains less battery.
- Landing at the rim is never fiddly; walls never knock you around.
