# 116 — Great Caves, Crystal Trophies and Cave Rock

**Build & start:** fresh `builds/windows/SomethingDownThere.exe`, **New Game** (older saves no longer
load). Quicker: title menu → **Ground Lab**: a great cave under the lab, its roof about 15 m down,
with a shaft from the surface to a metre above it (west of the bays; the prompt names it): drop down
and dig through the last metre. Four mini caves lie about 2 m down under patches of geode stone west,
north and south-east of the bays; a "Cave rock" bay shows the great caves' stone.

In the site every zone has one great cave (about 30, 60, 97 and 135 m down) and three mini caves
from about 8 m down. Developer admin → **Ground X-ray** marks cave rock yellow.

**Try:**
- Break into a cave or a geode: the first cut through drops a good piece of its roof or wall at once,
  with clods and dust. Digging on inside it throws no more debris.
- Inside an opened cave: dark but for its light sources. Under the hole you dropped through, a pool
  of light fades out over a few metres; beyond it only the crystals (each lighting the stone round it,
  no bright spot inside the crystal) and your lamps.
- Crystals where daylight reaches (a mini cave or geode dug open near the surface, a trophy at camp):
  no light on the stone, but still bright, coloured crystals rather than dark ones. Mini caves are
  back in grey geode stone.
- An extractable item (the crystal trophy, a computer): aim at it where soil still covers it nearby
  and dig, and the soil round that spot comes away; aimed where it is bare, nothing is dug, so you
  walk round it for the rest.
- A great cave is smaller now, one crystal colour, no arches, with its one big crystal trophy on the
  floor. Dig round its base, hold Interact anywhere on it: the crane hauls it up and sets it upright
  beside the camp.
- Geodes near the surface: their crystals should no longer paint the lit walls.

**Tell the agent:**
- The cave's darkness and the pool of light under the hole; crystals in light and in the dark.
- The digging assist on extractable items: too much, too little.
- The break-in: is the opening big enough, too big?
- The great caves' size and the trophy trip to camp.
