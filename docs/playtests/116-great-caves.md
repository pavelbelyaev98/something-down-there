# 116 — Great Caves, Crystal Trophies and Cave Rock

**Build & start:** fresh `builds/windows/SomethingDownThere.exe`, **New Game** (older saves no longer
load). Quicker: title menu → **Ground Lab**: a great cave under the lab, its roof about 15 m down,
with a shaft from the surface to a metre above it (west of the bays; the prompt names it): drop down
and dig through the last metre. Four mini caves lie about 2 m down under patches of cave rock west,
north and south-east of the bays.

In the site every zone has one great cave (about 30, 60, 97 and 135 m down) and three mini caves
from about 8 m down. Developer admin → **Ground X-ray** marks cave rock yellow.

**Try:**
- Break into a cave or a geode: the first cut through drops a good piece of its roof or wall at once,
  with clods and dust. Digging on inside it throws no more debris.
- Inside an opened cave: not pitch black any more, a faint light on the stone so you can make out its
  shape, far crystals no longer lone red dots in black; crystals glow and light the stone round them,
  but only in the dark (where daylight reaches, as in a geode dug open near the surface, they neither
  glow nor light anything, and a trophy at camp doesn't glow).
- A great cave is smaller now, one crystal colour, no arches, with its one big crystal trophy on the
  floor. Dig round its base, hold Interact anywhere on it: the crane hauls it up and sets it upright
  beside the camp.
- Geodes near the surface: their crystals should no longer paint the lit walls.

**Tell the agent:**
- Whether the caves now read in the dark (too dark, too bright), and the crystals' light.
- The break-in: is the opening big enough, too big?
- The great caves' size and the trophy trip to camp.
