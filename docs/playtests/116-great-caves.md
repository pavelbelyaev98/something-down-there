# 116 — Great Caves, Crystal Trophies and Cave Rock

**Build & start:** fresh `builds/windows/SomethingDownThere.exe`, **New Game** (older saves no longer
load). Quicker: title menu → **Ground Lab**: a great cave under the whole lab, its roof about 15 m
down, with a shaft from the surface to a metre above it (west of the bays; the prompt names it): drop
down and dig through the last metre. Four mini caves lie about 2 m down under patches of cave rock
west, north and south-east of the bays.

In the site every zone has one great cave (about 30, 60, 97 and 135 m down) and three mini caves
from about 8 m down. Developer admin → **Ground X-ray** marks cave rock yellow. Admin levels and
**Return to surface** help with the depth.

**Try:**
- Break into a great cave: a long hall almost as wide as the site, with stone pillars, arches and
  stalagmites, in the demo's purple-grey rock. Its four colour areas each hold crystals of one kind
  that glow like small lamps (bring a lamp to see the hall).
- Find the zone's crystal trophy standing on the floor, dig round its base until you can mark it,
  and hold Interact: the lifting eye goes on its top. The crane hauls it up and it stands upright
  beside the camp (seven spots now). Interact there reads its card.
- Mini caves: one small room with a few crystals of one kind.
- Dig into a cave from above with the shovel: no thin sheets or bits of stone should be left hanging
  across the opening.
- Walk around the Ground Lab: the stutter should be gone (crystals in sealed caves no longer light).

**Good feels like:** breaking into a great dark hall and finding the big crystal in it; the trophies
collecting beside the camp.

**Tell the agent:**
- The great caves' size, how often they come and what fills them; the cave rock's look.
- The trophies: how hard they are to dig free, the crane trip, how they stand at camp.
- Whether anything still hangs in the air after digging, and whether the lab still stutters.
