# 089 — Lighter dig plot

**Build & start:** fresh `builds/windows/SomethingDownThere.exe`, New Game (or Continue).

**Try:**
- Stand on the plot and look across the tape toward the crane and the camp.
- Walk out past the tape and look back at the plot.

**Good feels like:** the untouched plot is a light mud, only a touch darker than the ground around it.
Past the tape the ground turns light within a few steps, and green grass patches ring the plot a few
metres out (fewer by the camp). The old deep dark brown is gone everywhere. The damp ground by the
water is a little lighter too (it shares the band's colour).

**Compare (A/B):** Developer admin (Ctrl+Shift+F10) → **Boundary** steps the plot's fence for the session.
All use the double tape: Double tape (light stakes, default), Heavy posts (10 cm posts in concrete
footings), Post and rail (a timber rail along the post tops), Braced posts (a raking brace staked behind each
post). Stand close to a post, look along the line from inside the plot, then from the camp.

**Tell the agent:**
- Plot shade: right, lighter still, or a touch darker?
- Is the lighter ground starting at the right distance? More or less green, and where?
- Which fence to keep? Solid enough, or too heavy anywhere?
- Grass colour everywhere: warm muted olive close to the mud now. Too yellow, too grey, or right?
- Plants around the site should look like one style now (bushes and rock-top grass match the grass, all
  sway gently). Anything still frozen, waving too much, or off-colour?
