# Playtest 099 — Disturbed Ground, Odd Spots & Detector-Off Test

**On hold:** the site has one plain ground for now (no tells to follow); play this once the grounds
return.

**Build & start:** launch `SomethingDownThere.exe -noDetector` and start a New Game (or Ctrl+Shift+F10
→ Detector: OFF right after you spawn). **No help**: no X-ray, no developer shortcuts, no spec.

## Try
- Play normally and look for the three unique computers (they sit at about 8, 13.5 and 21 m).
- Notice whether you follow a tell on your own: a backfill pit, a gravel channel or a crack.

## Good feels like
- Backfill: a dark, chunky patch cutting across the banding; the tool suddenly sinks in; follow it
  and something always waits at the bottom. "Why did it just get easy? Someone dug here before me."
- Each unique sits in ground unlike its zone: a grey-blue clay lens in the brown soil.

## Tell the agent
- **Pass or fail:** pass = you found every unique without help and followed at least one tell
  unprompted, so the detector is removed. Fail = say what went wrong; the detector stays frozen.
