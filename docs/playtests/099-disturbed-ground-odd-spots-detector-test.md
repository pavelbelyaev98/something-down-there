**On hold:** play this once every unique sits in its lens of odd ground (`112`). Until then the only
tell to follow is a backfill pit.

**Build & start:** launch `SomethingDownThere.exe -noDetector` and start a New Game (or Ctrl+Shift+F10
→ Detector: OFF right after you spawn). **No help**: no X-ray, no developer shortcuts, no spec.

## Try
- Play normally and look for the three unique computers (they sit at about 8, 13.5 and 21 m).
- Notice whether you follow a tell on your own: a backfill pit, or a patch of odd ground in a wall.

## Good feels like
- Backfill: a messy, stony patch cutting across the soil; the tool feels the change at once; follow it
  and something always waits at the bottom. "This ground is different; someone dug here before me."
- Each unique sits in ground that does not belong there: the odd one out is the clue.
