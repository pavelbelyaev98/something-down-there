# 089 — Natural Drained Lakebed

**Build & start:** fresh `builds/windows/SomethingDownThere.exe`, New Game.

**Try:**
- Climb the big boulder south-east of the crane (where your screenshot was taken) and look out over the
  lakebed toward the water.
- Walk along both streams: the one north of the plot down to where it splits into two mouths at the lake,
  and the one south of the camp.
- Look at the stones in and beside the streams, the reeds and rushes at the water, and the grass patches on
  the flats and around the plot.
- Fly up to the flight ceiling and look at how the streams cross the lakebed.

**Good feels like:** a lake that drained recently: water still trickling from the cliff foot straight down
into the lake, creeks that bend unevenly with stony insides and steeper outsides, stones lying where the water
left them, plants growing where it is damp. Nothing reads as placed, outlined or evenly spaced. The plot and
the lighter ground around it are unchanged.

**Tell the agent:**
- Anything that still looks artificial (a screenshot helps), and whether the rocks are now too dark or too
  plain.
