# Playtest 006 — Material Zones & Ground Places

**Build & start:** current build, New Game. Best played with normal purchases for at least the first
two zones; developer shortcuts are fine for going deeper.
Developer admin → **Ground X-ray** marks cracks red, gravel yellow, backfill magenta, pond clay cyan and concrete white through the ground.

## Try
- Dig down through all four zones: soil with gravel lenses, orange clay with grey-blue bands from
  about 37.5 m, grey rock with rust-red clay veins from about 75 m, a colder placeholder rock from
  about 112.5 m.
- Dig sideways at a zone border to see it from the side.
- Look for the places: rubble in the soil, open concrete structures, rock masses, and softer
  pond-clay basins in the clay.
- In rock, follow a red clay vein instead of digging straight through.

## Good feels like
- Each zone feels like its own place, not the same stack repeated.
- Arriving in a new zone after one tool purchase never feels slower than the zone before.
- Rock is never a wall: a softer vein is always there to follow.
- Borders are short, wavy mixed bands, not flat lines.

## Tell the agent
- Any zone that felt like a restart or a wall, and any colour that reads badly (clay, concrete
  glare, the zone-4 placeholder).
