# Playtest — Rim Winch Look (A/B)

**Build & start:** current build, New Game or Continue. Stay at the camp. Facing the hole from the
spawn, **A** stands on the right past the exhibit stand, **B** on the left past the computer.

## Try
- Walk around both machines, then look at them from the far side of the hole and from inside your
  hole looking up: each attachment hangs high, 4–5 m out over the hole, where the rope would go down.
- Both are props, standing still in their "over the hole" pose. They are built to slew (A's whole top
  turns on its cross base; B's turret turns and its jib tilts), so once wired the load comes up and
  swings round onto a pad. Marking a find still uses the old yellow block.

## Good feels like
- A believable, slightly overbuilt worksite machine that obviously hauls things up by rope, readable
  from across the site and from the bottom of the hole; big enough for oversized salvage (A's top
  wheel about 16 m up, B's jib tip about 14 m) while ladders, railings and levers stay person-sized.

## Compare (A/B)
- **A, slewing tripod hoist:** fixed cross base on timber pads, chained to stakes; on top a turning
  deck with the electric winch, control post, sandbags and a tall tripod leaning out on two wire
  backstays. The rope runs under a low wheel, over the top wheel, down to a small lifting magnet.
- **B, trailer crane:** blue tandem-axle trailer on outrigger legs with a railed operator platform at
  the front; on it a turning diesel turret with the drum winch and a long yellow lattice jib on two
  ropes. The rope drops from the jib tip to a single suction cup with a small vacuum canister.
- **Attachments:** small (about 0.6 m) and stick to the find instead of hooking it. The magnet holds
  metal only (not a bone); the suction cup seals onto any smooth face. Either fits either machine.

## Tell the agent
- Which machine and attachment win, whether size and reach feel right, where loads should be set down
  once it swings, and anything to change. The winner replaces the old block; the other is removed.
