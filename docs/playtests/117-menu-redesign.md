# 117 — Menu Redesign (Title, Pause, Settings, HUD, Shop)

**Build & start:** fresh `builds/windows/SomethingDownThere.exe`. Look at the title first, then Continue or New
Game for the pause menu.

**Try:**
- Title: the crane over the dig site behind a big stacked logo. Hover the entries and move through them with
  the arrow keys; open Settings and come back.
- Settings: every tab. Change values with the ◀ ▶ arrows, Left/Right, Enter and clicks on the value. Watch the
  right-hand panel follow the row you are on. Rebind a key. Change Window mode (Keep/Revert should still appear).
- Esc out of settings, then press Esc in game for the pause menu: the game's name and the menu, laid out like
  the title, everything left-aligned.
  Main Menu saves and brings you back to the title; Continue picks up where you left.
- HUD while digging: coin and money top-left; the battery bar (bolt inside) with backpack and lamp bottom-left.
  Do the icons read as what they are? Dig the battery down and watch
  the bar turn orange, then red, with LOW BATTERY in the middle. Fill the bag: its count turns orange.
- The crosshair is a small dot. Dig out a rock or coal: no words on it at any point, just a small "+Coal"
  above the bag as you take it (two quick ones read "+Coal ×2"). Chest coins still say "E to take".
- Computer with a few finds: one row per kind (Rock ×3) and only Sell all. Then buy an upgrade, a recharge
  and a lamp. Tab shows the bag the same way. Ctrl+Shift+F10: the developer admin in the same style.
- Pause → New Game: it asks first; Cancel (or Esc) goes back to pause. Confirm on a game you don't mind losing
  (it is archived, not deleted): a fresh world starts straight away.

**Good feels like:** the title reads like a game's front door, not a debug panel; settings feel calm and
self-explaining: you never have to guess what an option does or which choices it has.
