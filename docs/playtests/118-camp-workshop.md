# 118 — Camp Workshop With the Computer Inside

**Build & start:** fresh `builds/windows/SomethingDownThere.exe`, Continue or New Game. The workshop stands
south-west of the camp, its roller door open toward it.

**Try:**
- From the camp and from the dig plot's tape: does the workshop sit right in the camp, and can you see the
  computer through the door?
- Walk in and use the computer at the back right: sell a haul, buy an upgrade. Is the walk from the hole
  short enough for every trip?
- Look round inside: sun through the milky roof sheets, the tube lights, racking and workbench. Up close:
  corrugated sheets, rust, timber, concrete.
- Walk round it, step onto the slab, jetpack onto the roof.

**Good feels like:** a real worksite shed where you go to cash in and, later, keep your best finds: solid,
worn, and lit well enough inside to see things clearly.

**Tell the agent:**
- Computer spot inside the workshop: keep it, or move it (nearer the door, another wall)?
- Anything that looks fake up close, too dark inside, or makes the trip to the computer feel long.
