# 110 — Geodes

**Build & start:** fresh `builds/windows/SomethingDownThere.exe`, **New Game** (older saves no longer
load). Five geodes lie between 37 and 112 m down. Developer admin → **Ground X-ray** marks their
shells cyan; admin levels and **Return to surface** help with the depth. Quicker: title menu →
**Ground Lab**, the **Geode** bay (a big geode about 2 m down) and the **Geode shell** bay beside it.

**Try:**
- Dig into a geode's shell: the dark grey stone, taking small, hard bites (about a quarter of soil's
  speed). With the drill it should still go through in seconds.
- Inside: an uneven hollow a few metres across, bulging into one or two side pockets, big enough to
  stand in. Ten crystals of one kind line it: celestine or fluorite in the upper geodes, amethyst or
  citrine in the deeper ones ($30–75 each).
- Drill aim: stand over a small hole into a geode and point just inside its rim. The drill should bore
  the rim around you, not try for the floor far below.

**Good feels like:** "a hard ball of rock... something is in there", then darkness, a lamp, and a
room of one colour of crystal.

**Tell the agent:**
- Whether the size and shape feel like a find, and whether ten crystals fill it.
- Whether the drill now digs the edge you meant.
