# 110 — Geodes

**Build & start:** fresh `builds/windows/SomethingDownThere.exe`, **New Game** (older saves no longer
load). Five geodes lie between 37 and 112 m down. Developer admin → **Ground X-ray** marks their
shells cyan; admin levels and **Return to surface** help with the depth. Quicker: title menu →
**Ground Lab**, the **Geode** bay (a whole geode 2.6 m down) and the **Geode shell** bay beside it.

**Try:**
- Dig into a geode's shell: the dark grey stone you picked, taking small, hard bites (about a quarter
  of soil's speed). With the drill it should still go through in seconds.
- Break through: crumbs and a little dust fall into a dark hollow. Light it with a lamp.
- Six crystals line it, pointing in from the floor and walls: celestine and fluorite in the upper
  geodes, amethyst and citrine in the deeper ones. Dig them free and sell them ($40–110 each).

**Good feels like:** "a hard ball of rock... something is in there", then darkness, a lamp, and a
sparkle that pays.

**Tell the agent:**
- Whether the shell's toll feels like seconds, and whether a geode pays enough for the detour.
- Whether you'd want geodes to do more (see the agent's ideas in chat).
