# Playtest 001 — Evolving Motorized Tool Rig

**Build & start:** current build, New Game. Buy Tool levels at the computer, or use Ctrl+Shift+1…9, 0
to preview every level.

## Try
- Dig soil, gravel, clay, rock and concrete with the shovel, then with the drill (level 7+).
- Hold a find, place a lamp, mark a unique for recovery: the tool should get out of the way.
- Start a shovel stroke and turn to another spot before the scoop: the dirt should come out where you look
  when the blade lifts, not where the stroke began.
- Change the field of view in Settings; crouch into a tight tunnel and dig against its walls.
- At a drill level, Developer admin (Ctrl+Shift+F10): **Drill position** starts at -6 cm and goes on to
  -12 cm a centimetre at a time (then -5 to 0); **Drill size** starts at 100% and steps 105, 110, 115, 120,
  130, 140, 150, 175, 200, 90 and 95%.
- Hold the drill on one spot: its tip should open the middle and the hole widen around it into a clean
  cone within about a second (no little pit at the bottom), while shovel scoops stay flat. Drill at a
  slant: the hole should go in the way you look, not straight down. Dig into a thin bridge or overhang
  from below: it should give.

## Good feels like
- One shovel at every shovel level and the hand mining drill from level 7, nothing bolted on.
- Each stroke pries the soil loose and scoops it, the dirt going as the blade lifts (harder ground
  pushes it less); the drill's head spins while cutting; the
  camera itself never moves.
- It rises from the bottom-right corner and takes little space; it never covers the crosshair or
  what you are digging, and never sticks through a wall.

## Tell the agent
- The winning **Drill position** and **Drill size**, and how the drill sits on screen.
- Does the drill's cone read right, and does it widen evenly? Does its slow sweep over fresh ground matter?
- Size and placement, motion, and clipping.
