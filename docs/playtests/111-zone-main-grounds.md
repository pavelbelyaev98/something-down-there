# 111 — Zone Main Grounds

**Build & start:** fresh `builds/windows/SomethingDownThere.exe`, **New Game** (older saves no longer
load). Zone 2 (from about 37 m) is now old grey lake sediment, zone 3 (from about 75 m) the old
riverbed, now a pebbled stone in the cave rock's style (lighter, so caves still stand out); zone 4 is
still soil. Quicker: title menu → **Ground Lab**: a **Lake
sediment** bay (south row, east end), and north-east a **Riverbed** bay and a **Zone borders** bay
(soil, sediment from about 3.5 m, riverbed from about 8 m). Bring a lamp; below 20 m only lamps light.

**Try:**
- Dig down through the 37 m and 75 m borders with the tool you would own there (admin levels 4 and 7):
  the ground changes over a few metres in patches, and the border is never one flat line.
- Dig a while in each ground. Sediment: a little firmer than soil, smooth, with thin level bands in
  the walls. Riverbed: stony, smaller bites with the short bite motion, the drill keeping up.
- Iron, silver and pyrite favour the sediment, gold and emerald the riverbed.
- Dig straight down a while, looking at the floor: every third of a metre or so the stones change, never the same
  pattern sliding down with you (also when tunnelling sideways).

**Good feels like:** "it's different down here", and the tool still keeps up.

**Compare (A/B):** Developer admin → **Zone grounds** (session only, also in the lab):
- **Lake sediment look:** A grey silt / B blue-grey clay / C puddled mud.
- **Riverbed look (new):** A conglomerate / B brown conglomerate / C grit.
- **Zone grounds dig:** authored / like soil / firmer (the Ground tuning sliders refine either).
- **Zone 4 (next New Game):** soil / riverbed carried down to the bottom. Set it in the Ground Lab, then
  **Leave Ground Lab** and start a New Game (quitting the game resets it).

**Tell the agent:**
- Your pick for each look, and whether the dig feel passes (or which preset).
- Whether zone 4 should stay soil or keep the riverbed until its own ground arrives.
- Anything glaring, too pale or too close to geode shell or cave rock (the riverbed shares the cave rock's
  style on purpose: say if a great cave in zone 3 no longer stands out).
