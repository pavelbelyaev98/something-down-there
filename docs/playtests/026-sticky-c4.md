# 026 — Sticky C4

**Build & start:** fresh `builds/windows/SomethingDownThere.exe`, **New Game** (older saves no longer load). The two
new keys reset your custom key bindings once. Quicker: developer admin (Ctrl+Shift+F10) → **Add $500** and **Fill C4
pack**, or the title menu's **Ground Lab** (its geode bay is the natural target).

**Try:**
- Buy C4 at the computer: the Services column sells single charges, the Upgrades column has the C4 track (bigger
  blasts, more charges, cheaper charges).
- Press **G**: a ghost charge and an orange ball show where it sticks and the ground its blast takes. Aim at a find,
  the fence, the sky or with no charges left: it turns red and says why; nothing is spent.
- Click to throw it: it flies straight there and sticks, blinking. Throw a few more, walk away, press **B**: they go
  off one after another.
- Blast soil, the backfill pits and a geode's shell (the shell cracks open wider). Look at what is left: finds, chests,
  lamps and their light, the crystal cavern's clusters.
- Dig the ground away under an armed charge, or pick one back up with **E**.

**Good feels like:** properly powerful and predictable: the crater is the ball you saw, a charge clearly worth its
price against digging, and a geode cracked open in one go.

**Tell the agent:**
- Whether a charge is worth its price and when you would first buy one (the track arrives by price, not a lock).
- Blast size at the start and at the top of the track; the flash and debris; the stand-in charge's look.
