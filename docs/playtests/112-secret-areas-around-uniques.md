# 112 — Gravel Around the Computers

**Build & start:** fresh `builds/windows/SomethingDownThere.exe`, **New Game**. Turn the detector off
first (Developer admin → **Detector: OFF**) and leave Ground X-ray off. The three computers lie
between about 8 and 21 m down.

**What it is:** the river gravel you saw round the computers is the whole feature. It is a clue: each
patch is about 10 m wide, much wider than the computer, and flat like a pancake: about 3 m thick at
the computer, thinning to nothing at its edge. So you should meet the gravel in a wall before you see
the computer, then follow it to the computer.

**Try:**
- Dig the first 25 m your own way, sideways as well as down, with a lamp.
- When a wall shows a strip of gravel across the soil, follow the strip where it gets thicker.
- Dig into gravel in the dark: the tool takes smaller bites, so you can feel it without light.

**Good feels like:** "that's not the same ground... something's in there."

**Tell the agent:**
- Did you hit the gravel before you saw the computer, and did following it lead you there?
- Pass or fail: did you meet the gravel often enough? If not, a thin gravel trail running out from
  each patch toward the middle of the plot is the next step.
- Did the gravel ever look like a backfill pit?
