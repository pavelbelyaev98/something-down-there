# 112 — Lenses Around Uniques

**Build & start:** fresh `builds/windows/SomethingDownThere.exe`, **New Game** (older saves keep the
old ground). Turn the detector off first (Developer admin → **Detector: OFF**) so the HUD gives
nothing away, and leave Ground X-ray off. The three computers lie between about 8 and 21 m down.

Each computer now sits in a **lens of river gravel**: a flat patch about 10 m across of the stony
riverbed ground inside the soil, about 3 m thick at the computer and thinning to nothing at its edge.

**Try:**
- Dig the first 25 m your own way, sideways as well as down, with a lamp. When a wall shows a band of
  stony ground across the soil, follow it where it gets thicker.
- Dig into the band in the dark: the tool takes smaller bites, so you can feel it without light.
- Count how many shafts or tunnels you dug before you met a lens, and whether you found every
  computer this way.
- To check one on purpose: Ground X-ray marks lenses green.

**Good feels like:** "that's not the same ground... something's in there."

**Tell the agent:**
- Pass or fail: did you meet the lenses often enough? If not, a trail of the same ground leading out
  from each lens toward the plot's middle is the next step.
- Did a lens ever look like a backfill pit (both are stony and slower)?
- Did "follow it where it thickens" work, or did you lose the band?
