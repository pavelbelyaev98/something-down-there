# Playtest 100 — Ground Lab, slumps, crack breaks, new tool ladder

**Build & start:** current build, title menu → **Ground Lab** (development build only). Unlimited
battery is on; Ctrl+Shift+1…9, 0 picks a tool level; aim at a bay to read its name. Nothing is saved;
pause → Leave Ground Lab returns to the title.

## Try
- Row nearest you: soil, gravel, backfill, clay, pond clay, rock. Dig each at level 1 and level 7.
- Middle row: concrete, shattered rock/concrete, rock with cracks (dig into a pale seam), concrete
  with a crack, rock with a clay vein.
- Back row: dig under the gravel layer (pour), undercut the backfill pit (slump), dig down through
  the thin soil roof (slump) and the thin clay roof (holds), feel the soil/clay/rock layers.
- Developer admin → Ground tuning: pick a ground, move its sliders, feel it, Print ground tuning.

## Good feels like
- Each ground is recognisable with eyes closed: bite size, a little stroke speed, its own chips/dust.
- A crack bite breaks a chunk loose along the seam; loose ground drops in one harmless rush.
- Level 1 feels like a real shovel; each purchase is a clear step; the drill (7) is a jump.
- The tool shows its blade and does a small thrust, never a big swing.

## Tell the agent
- Which grounds still feel alike, printed ground tuning you like, and whether slumps or crack breaks
  are too big or too small.
