# 052 — Fewer Rocks Below the Surface

**Build & start:** fresh `builds/windows/SomethingDownThere.exe`, **New Game** (the rocks are placed
when a game starts; a continued save keeps its old ones).

**Try:**
- Scrape the first few centimetres: as many rocks as before right under the turf.
- Dig on down to about 5 m: from about 0.7 m down there are now roughly half as many rocks as before,
  so coal and copper show up more among them.

**Good feels like:** the first scrape still turns up rocks, and the metres below it no longer feel
crowded with them.

**Tell the agent:**
- Right amount now, or still too many or too few between 1 and 5 m down?
