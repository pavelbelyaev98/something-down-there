# 118 — Camp Shelters: Workshop or Tent

**Build & start:** fresh `builds/windows/SomethingDownThere.exe`, Continue or New Game. Both shelters stand
beside the camp: turn round from the computer.

**Try:**
- Walk up to each from the computer, then from the dig plot's tape: how does each sit in the camp?
- Go inside both. Look along the back wall, the racking or the table, and up at the roof: the workshop's
  milky roof sheets let the sun in, the tent's canvas glows a little and its pendant lamps light the pallets.
- Walk round them, step onto the slab and the pallets, jetpack onto both roofs.
- Look close at the materials: corrugated sheets, rust, timber, concrete; canvas seams and patches, ropes,
  pallets.

**Good feels like:** a real worksite building you would keep your best finds in: solid, worn, and lit well
enough inside that things put there would be easy to see.

**Compare (A/B):** the green corrugated workshop (south-west) and the olive canvas frame tent (west).

**Tell the agent:**
- Which one stays: workshop or tent? (The other is removed.)
- Anything that looks fake up close, too dark inside, or in the way of the camp.
