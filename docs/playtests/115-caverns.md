# 115 — Caves and a Wider Underground

**Build & start:** fresh `builds/windows/SomethingDownThere.exe`, **New Game** (older saves no longer
load). Quicker: title menu → **Ground Lab**: four small caves about 2 m down, one of each crystal kind,
two west of the bays and two north of them (the prompt names them when you aim there).

In the site, caves come every few metres of depth from about 10 m down, three in each zone, all
under the plot. Developer admin → **Ground X-ray** marks their stone cyan, like a geode's. Admin
levels and **Return to surface** help with the depth.

**Try:**
- Dig down until you break into a cave through its hard stone. Its crystals glow in their colour
  and light it: one kind to a cave (blue quartz near the top, amber cubes, green hex prisms, ruby
  at the bottom). Take them like any find; the cave's light dims as you do.
- Keep digging into the cave (or a chest's pocket, or a geode) after you break in: each cut that
  opens it more drops its clods and dust inside.
- Walk around in it: the floor, the domed roof.
- Geodes: their crystals now glow and light the hollow too (see the 110 note).
- Underground the site is wider than the plot: tunnel sideways under the edge.

**Good feels like:** digging down and breaking into a small glowing room of one colour, every few
metres, a little reward on the way down.

**Tell the agent:**
- Whether the caves come often enough and feel worth breaking into, and their size.
- Whether the glow and light look right now (no white blow-out).
- Whether the take feels right for the trip, and the break-in debris as you keep digging.
