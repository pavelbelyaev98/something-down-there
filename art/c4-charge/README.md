# C4 charge (stand-in)
- **Item:** the armed C4 charge: a demolition block with a red arming LED and light, plus the blast preview ball's shader.
- **Purpose:** the charges the player sticks on the ground and sets off (`026`); the preview shows the ball a blast takes.
- **Source/License:** the Mining pack's flat ingot mesh and silver ingot normal map (approved pack, `art/mining-pack`) in a project olive-drab material; the LED is Unity's built-in cube at 12 mm; `Runtime/Interaction/BlastPreview.shader` is original. No new model: a proper charge belongs to the art chat.
- **Unity path:** `Assets/Content/C4` (`C4Charge.prefab`, `C4 Block.mat`, `C4 LED.mat`, `C4 Blast Preview.mat`), built by **Configure C4 Charges**.
- **Status:** stand-in, in MainGame.
