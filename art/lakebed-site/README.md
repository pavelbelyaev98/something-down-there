# Asset: Lakebed Site (generated)
- **Item:** Code-generated lakebed art: the sculpted and painted terrain data, the rim collar, the lake surface, the stream water mesh, the dig plot's edge-distance map (layout data for the ground shader's blend) and the dig boundary meshes (a merged ring of pack stones, stakes with barrier tape, timber edging) with plain tape materials.
- **Purpose:** The drained lakebed around the dig plot, regenerated from the unmodified Highlands demo by `LakebedSiteSetup`.
- **Source/License:** Original project geometry and terrain edits made in the Editor. Textures and scenery come from the approved packs; their cards list the tinted material, layer and plant copies (`art/pure-nature-highlands`, `art/pure-nature-mountains`).
- **Unity Path:** `unity/Assets/Content/Lakebed/` (`LakebedTerrain.asset`, `ExcavationRim.asset`, `LakeSurface.asset`, `Trickles.asset`, `DigEdgeDistance.asset`, `Boundary/`, `BoundaryTapeRed.mat`, `BoundaryTapeWhite.mat`).
- **Status:** In use. Rerun **Configure Lakebed Excavation Site** rather than editing these by hand; see `unity/readme.md`.
