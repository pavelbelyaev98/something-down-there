# Lifting eye
- **Item:** swivel lifting point: bolted flange (`Base`), ball swivel and forged bow (`Swivel`), sized for the salvage crane's hook; baked worn powder coat, bare steel, zinc, rust, dust and mud.
- **Purpose:** bolted onto a unique where the player holds the mark; the crane's hook rides the rope into its ring.
- **Source/License:** original project geometry created through Blender MCP; no external content.
- **Source files:** `create_assets.py` (geometry, procedural shaders, Cycles bakes) and `lifting-eye.blend` reproduce `LiftingEye.fbx` and its maps.
- **Unity path:** `Assets/Content/Salvage/Models/LiftingEye.fbx`; maps `Assets/Content/Salvage/Textures/LiftingEye_Albedo/_Normal/_Mask.png` (mask: metallic R, occlusion G, smoothness A); URP material `Assets/Content/Salvage/LiftingEye.mat`; wired by **Configure Salvage Crane**.
- **Status:** integrated in MainGame.
