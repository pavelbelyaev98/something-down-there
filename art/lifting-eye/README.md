# Lifting eye
- **Item:** swivel lifting eye (base plate, hex collar, painted ring), sized for the salvage crane's hook.
- **Purpose:** bolted onto a unique where the player holds the mark; the crane's hook rides the rope into its ring.
- **Source/License:** original project geometry created through Blender MCP; no external content.
- **Source files:** `create_assets.py` and `lifting-eye.blend` reproduce `LiftingEye.fbx`.
- **Unity path:** `Assets/Content/Salvage/Models/LiftingEye.fbx`; URP materials `Assets/Content/Salvage/LiftingEyePaint.mat` and `LiftingEyeSteel.mat`; wired by **Configure Salvage Crane**.
- **Status:** integrated in MainGame.
