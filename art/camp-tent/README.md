# Camp tent
- **Item:** a 9 × 5 m olive-drab canvas frame tent: galvanised 48 mm tube frame, sagging roof with seams and patches, valance, gable ends, rolled walls strapped up, pleated back wall, guy ropes with runners and stakes, a Euro-pallet floor, a trestle table and two enamel pendant lamps.
- **Purpose:** the camp's stash shelter (A/B against `art/camp-workshop`, playtest `118`).
- **Source/License:** original project geometry and textures made through Blender MCP from real reference photos (Wikimedia Commons, not shipped). No external content.
- **Source files:** `tent_geometry.py` (parts), `create_assets.py` (UVs, Cycles bakes, FBX; uses `art/camp-workshop/bakekit.py` and its geometry helper), `make_weave.py` (tiling canvas weave normal), `preview.py`, `camp-tent.blend`.
- **Unity path:** `Assets/Content/Camp/Tent/` (`Models/Tent.fbx`, `Textures/`, `Materials/` on Excavation Lit, `Tent.json` layout); placed by **Configure Camp Shelters**.
- **Status:** in MainGame for the A/B playtest.
