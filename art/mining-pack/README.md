# Asset: Mining Tools, Ore & Ingots
- **Item:** REAL DEDICATED low-poly mining props with LODs: ore chunks A/B (copper, iron, silver, gold), flat and thin ingots, octagon and round coins, a shovel, pickaxe and mallet, rock sets and mineral-deposit decals.
- **Purpose:** The plain rock (`rock.json`: its rounded rocks in the pack's own stone, `Rock_Rounded_*`); the coal, copper, iron, silver and gold minerals (`art/minerals`: copper its ore chunks; coal on its layered rocks; silver and gold native nuggets on its jagged rocks, gold's flattened; iron grey ore stone with rust in its cracks on the same rocks); the old chests' bronze, silver and gold ingots (`catalog.json`, taken by hand). Its coins (messy in a chest), copper ingots (bronze reads apart from gold) and its shovel (the Western one won) were tried and dropped (113).
- **Source/License:** User-purchased [Asset Store pack](https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/3d/props/exterior/mining-tools-ore-ingots-26357) (2026-10-06), Unity Asset Store EULA (Extension Asset); keep source access restricted.
- **Unity Path:** `unity/Assets/REAL_DEDICATED/` (vendor files untouched apart from a 1024 px import cap on the used maps). Its loose materials are HDRP. Configure Buried Props builds URP copies and prefab variants in `Content/BuriedProps/MiningPack`.
- **Repository:** The whole import is committed like the other packs, its binaries (`.tga`, `.fbx`, `.exr`, `.hdr`) in LFS. Its two nested installers (`*_BRP/_URP.unitypackage`, 1.9 GB) are ignored. The rocks coal and the nuggets use are project copies (their full-detail meshes in `MiningPack/Rocks`, copied by Configure Buried Props, and 1024 px maps written by `make_maps.py`), so the game never loads the 4K rock maps.
- **Status:** In the game (113, 114, 116: its jagged small rock's brown stone is also the great caves' cave rock, `Content/GroundTextures/CaveRock_*`, a clean square of its atlas made seamless and dark by `make_cave_rock.py`; the riverbed's three looks, `Content/GroundTextures/Riverbed{Conglomerate,Brown,Grit}_*`, are squares of its rounded rocks made seamless the same way and graded lighter by `make_riverbed.py`; both share `seamless.py`). The ingots are in the pack's colours, shiny but only part metal (`IngotMetal`, `IngotGloss`); copper ore is at the pack's quality on `Ore_Copper_Rich.png` and its mask (`make_maps.py`: the pack's copper grown over about half the stone). The nuggets take `Rocks/<set>_Metal.png` (the rock's light and dark only) in their metal's colour.
