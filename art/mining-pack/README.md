# Asset: Mining Tools, Ore & Ingots
- **Item:** REAL DEDICATED low-poly mining props with LODs: ore chunks A/B (copper, iron, silver, gold), flat and thin ingots, octagon and round coins, a shovel, pickaxe and mallet, rock sets and mineral-deposit decals.
- **Purpose:** The copper, iron, silver and gold minerals (`art/minerals`); the old chests' coins and ingots (`catalog.json`, taken by hand); the shovel as a developer-admin second look for levels 1-6.
- **Source/License:** User-purchased [Asset Store pack](https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/3d/props/exterior/mining-tools-ore-ingots-26357) (2026-10-06), Unity Asset Store EULA (Extension Asset); keep source access restricted.
- **Unity Path:** `unity/Assets/REAL_DEDICATED/` (vendor files untouched apart from a 1024 px import cap on the used maps). Its loose materials are HDRP. Configure Buried Props builds URP copies and prefab variants in `Content/BuriedProps/MiningPack`, and Configure Tool Rig builds the shovel's in `Content/ToolRig/MiningShovel`.
- **Repository:** Only the 65 files the game uses are committed (the vendor prefabs, materials, models and maps that `Content/BuriedProps/MiningPack` and `Content/ToolRig/MiningShovel` depend on, by `AssetDatabase.GetDependencies`), with the binaries in LFS. Its two nested installers (`*_BRP/_URP.unitypackage`, 1.9 GB) are ignored. The unused 4K rock sets, decals, `_Showcase` and `_Shared` stay out until they are needed.
- **Status:** In the game (113). Coins and ingots are tarnished copies (lower smoothness). Its round coins are unused (the user prefers the octagon ones).
