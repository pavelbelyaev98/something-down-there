# Camp workshop
- **Item:** an 8 × 6 m corrugated-iron workshop on a concrete slab: timber frame, faded green sheets with laps and rust, milky skylight sheets, windows, gutters and downpipe, a 4.6 m roller shutter, workbench with vice, slotted-angle racking, two fluorescent battens.
- **Purpose:** the camp's stash shelter (A/B against `art/camp-tent`, playtest `118`).
- **Source/License:** original project geometry and textures made through Blender MCP from real reference photos (Wikimedia Commons, not shipped); the slab's detail normal is the purchased Tower Crane pack's `concrete_Normal`. No other external content.
- **Source files:** `workshop_geometry.py` (parts), `create_assets.py` (UVs, Cycles bakes, FBX), `bakekit.py` (shared with the tent), `make_ribs.py` (tiling rib normal), `png8.py` (8-bit maps after bakes), `preview.py` (flat-colour form renders), `camp-workshop.blend`.
- **Unity path:** `Assets/Content/Camp/Workshop/` (`Models/Workshop.fbx`, `Textures/`, `Materials/` on Excavation Lit, `Workshop.json` layout); placed by **Configure Camp Shelters**.
- **Status:** in MainGame for the A/B playtest.
